## Done

### Fixes from the R-CDE review
- **M5:** while the throw is shown, `CrapsFlow.click` returns `REASON_ROLLING` and nothing is sent. `CrapsFlow.tool`
  (Undo / Clear) is off with `REASON_ROLLING`.
  - `CrapsScreen.takeBack()` routes right-click (`Press.right()`), `BetPort.remove` and `BetPort.tool` through the same gate. It
    flashes the reason instead of sending a packet.
  - Decision ACCEPTED by the coordinator: there is no server-side "no bets while rolling" gate.
- **M6:** the odds lane on the come-out returns `reason.odds_need_point` and never places a Pass bet. With a point on and no
  Pass bet it returns `reason.odds_need_pass`.
- **m2:** the bots' bet moment and bot removal call `markPublic()`.
- **m3:** `CrapsPub` uses the bot-aware seat index (`bots.occupants()`), so stacks and plates share a seat.
  - The bots' virtual bets (`botTable.shadow().bets()`) go after the players' stacks. Players' stacks are kept first when
    there are more than 12.
  - The bots' nets of the last roll are in `nets`.
  - Test accessor: `lastPublishTime()`.

### Other changes
- **Dice snap:** the dice ease from the throw's rest point into the `die1/die2` slots over 4 ticks (200 ms). CM replaces this
  with its own dice zone.
- **CB-G request (table presets):** `CrapsTableBlockEntity` now honours `TablePresetProvider` presets:
  - `minBet` raises the table minimum;
  - `tierMaxMultiplier` gives max = multiplier × tier max. The owner's max still applies, and the check is done the way
    High-Roller blackjack does it;
  - `minTier` gives `error.vip_required`.

  No payout, settle or RNG code changed.
- **Strings:** `reason.odds_need_point` is shortened: EN "Odds need a point first", RU "Сначала нужен поинт". The old text was
  truncated in the 480 status. Lang files were regenerated (no `--prune`).
- **`gametest/v2/craps/CrapsV2GameTests`** is now 5 real server tests. Players are connected through an EmbeddedChannel, open the
  table with `useBlock`, and act only through `TableNetworking.receive`:
  - `freshPlayerBetsPassOddsComeFieldByPacket`: an unseated player is seated by the Pass bet. Odds on the come-out are refused
    with a reason and cost 0, and the screen's flow gives `odds_need_point`. After point 8, odds 50, Come 10 and Field 10 each
    debit exactly their amount.
  - `undoAndClearTakeBackExactlyTheirChips`: Undo returns exactly the Field and the escrow drops by the same amount. Clear
    returns Come + odds. The contract Pass bet stays. A stray Undo gives `nothing_to_undo`.
  - `theThrowClosesTheFeltOnTheScreen`: on the state the server sent after the roll, every spot and Undo/Clear give
    `REASON_ROLLING` and nothing moves on the server. Once settled, the same state sends a Come bet, and it lands.
  - `botBetsOnTheBoardOnTheirSeats`: 2 bots. Their stacks are on the bot seats of the occupants header, the player's bets are on
    the player's seat, no stack is on seat -1, and the board is published after the bots bet.
  - `presetLimitsAreHonoured`: under a Lounge-like preset (min 100, 2× tier), 50 is refused with `bet_too_low` and costs 0. 100
    lands. A bet above 2× tier is refused. 1.5× tier lands. The screen state shows min 100 / max 2× tier.
- **`CrapsV2ClientGameTests` (real-flow):**
  - Sizes: 480 EN, 640 EN, 427 EN, 480 RU, 640 RU.
  - "Fresh" rounds (480 EN, 427 EN, 480 RU): no Sit and no chip picked; the PASS click alone seats the player and bets. The
    other rounds press Sit, then Roll without a line bet (gives the reason, no roll), then arm 25.
  - Come on the come-out is closed.
  - The odds lane on the come-out gives `odds_need_point` with no bet change.
  - During the first throw, a felt click and a right click give `REASON_ROLLING` and the server is unchanged.
  - Rolls use Roll and Space alternately; the balance is checked each roll against the server's returns.
  - After the point: odds lane → odds, then Come, then Field. `tool.undo` returns exactly the Field. A right click on the felt
    returns exactly the Come. Then roll to resolution.
  - In-world: the 4 FACINGs × 3 and 6 blocks, from the player side, plus one RU shot.

## Requests to other packages
- **A2 (baccarat) / K2:** `BaccaratPresets.preset()` calls `level.getBlockEntity(pos)`. When it is reached from
  `TableLifecycle.onChunkUnloading`, then `playOutNow`, `leave`, `syncViewers` (e.g. blackjack `isHighRoller` → `minBet`), it
  re-enters `DistanceManager` and crashes the server (NPE `ReferenceOpenHashSet` iterator; seen once in a 26.2 full run).
  Fix: cache the preset per table, or skip the lookup while `removing()`. Craps already does this
  (`CrapsTableBlockEntity.tablePreset()`).
- **W2:** the plate is hidden by the felt edge at 3 m (see above).
- **CB-G:** craps reads `preset().minBet / tierMaxMultiplier / minTier`. Give the Lounge craps a `TablePreset` (e.g.
  `high_roller_craps`, 100, 2, GOLD).
