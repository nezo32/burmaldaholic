# CR-2 handoff: village annex, Piglin Parlor, High Roller Lounge

Branch `p2/cr-2` (from `p2/cr-1`). Pieces only. Placement (`CasinoStructures`, `Geometry`, site probes, tunnels) belongs to WG.

## Done
- **Village annex** (`VillageAnnex`, `AnnexPalettes`, `BannerFont`): a 17x15x17 deco picture palace in 5 village styles. Twin vertical CASINO banner blades, a marquee canopy, all multi-block tables (baccarat included), and the snowy palette fix (white quartz/spruce, navy board). Passes R1–R6.
- **Piglin Parlor** (`PiglinParlor` on the MIT Bygone Nether Piglin Manor shell, `PiglinManorShell` generated by `java/tools/gen_parlor_shell.py`; licence in `META-INF/licenses/bygone-nether-MIT.txt`, CREDITS.md). Changes in R3:
  - Assumes no surrounding rock: the whole envelope is carved, and rows 0..1 are now solid foundation under the full footprint (the manor stamp had left air at the veranda edges). All lava is sealed inside the piece.
  - New 2-wide, 3-high **back door** (x 8..9, z 1) with jambs, a lintel, a slab hood and soul lanterns, plus a step outside. The front door is reached via the forecourt and the entrance stair.
- **High Roller Lounge** (`HighRollerLounge`): the goblet stem on the tower roof is gone. The building is now **ground-standing**:
  - a solid 4-row purpur skirt sits under the whole plate (pilasters every 6, end-stone courses, purpur band);
  - a 3-wide, 4-step front stair has structure void above the steps, so on higher ground the stair sinks into the terrain instead of cutting a trench.
- **Dealer cells**: every house table's dealer cell is 2 blocks of plain air on a solid floor (tested for the annex in all styles, the Parlor and the Lounge). The existing table-dealer NPC markers (Parlor baccarat/poker PIGLIN_DEALER, Lounge and annex BACCARAT_DEALER) stand in those cells as markers only. **DL**: replace or skip them to avoid double dealers.
