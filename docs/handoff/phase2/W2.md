## Done

### Phase 2 findings
- **M6 (bot tags):**
  - Deleted `BoardAwareTextDisplayRenderer` and the global `EntityRenderers.register(TEXT_DISPLAY…)` override.
  - New client mixin `bots/mixin/client/TextDisplayRendererMixin` (bots mixins json, `client` section). It injects at the
    TAIL of `DisplayRenderer.TextDisplayRenderer.extractRenderState(Display$TextDisplay, TextDisplayEntityRenderState, F)`.
    That descriptor is in both client jars (checked with javap on 26.2 and 26.3).
  - The rule lives in `bots/client/BotTagVisibility`: a bot plate over a table whose renderer is a board with a seat
    header is hidden, **at any distance** (VQA round 1 #8: one name system near tables). Every other text display is
    untouched.
  - The cache is weak-keyed and cleared on disconnect.
- **M7 (plates across tables):**
  - New pure `PlateLayout.TablePlates` + `resolveGlobal` (greedy by rank, hysteresis margin against other tables) and
    client `PlateFrame`.
  - Ranking: the seated table, then the looked-at one (any part, via `TableParts.mainOf`), then by distance. A plate
    that loses fades to 0 over 150 ms.
  - Each board is a fixed occupant for other tables' plates.
  - A frame rolls when a table submits a second time. `Minecraft.getFrameTimeNs()` is a frame DURATION, which caused
    the ghost plates in VQA round 1 #4.
- **Dealer obstacle:**
  - `BoardGeometry.dealerRect` / `BoardRenderer.dealerRect`; `CardTableRenderer` adds the Hold'em / UTH community row.
  - Obstacles and anchors are projected along the view ray (perspective), not orthographically.
  - The table top's NEAR edge is also an obstacle, so a plate never sinks behind the felt edge (D2's 3 m request).
- **m11:** no per-frame allocation in the plate path (static scratch, `PlateLayout.Scratch`, pooled `PlateRef`).
- **m12:** `BoardStats` is off unless `-Dburmaldaholic.boardStats=true` (tests turn it on); it clears on disconnect.
- **m14:** text size uses the camera's effective FOV.
- **LOD and sizes:**
  - LOD hysteresis is 1 block.
  - Text base is 1/60 (board) and 1/75 (plates). `readablePx` grows the text to the 18 → 13 px cap targets.
  - On-screen cap is now **capped at 22 px @1080p (44 @4K)** (VQA round 1 #3).
- **Contrast:** `HoloScale.PANEL` / `MUTED` / `contrast()`; the muted ink keeps ≥ 4.5:1 on the panel over any
  background (unit test).
- **Gear (R-AB M6):** `BotsClientModule` uses `V2TableScreen.registerSettings(be -> be instanceof BotTable, …)`; the
  deprecated hook is no longer set.
- **UI-A step 5:** `BoardKit.interim()` is empty.

### Multi-block tables (MT-DESIGN §2 "W2 interface change")
- The overhang code is deleted: `blocksOverhang`, `overhangCells`, `covers`, `BoardRenderer.blocked`.
- `BoardGeometry.fallback(felt, compact)`.
- **Full table (part ≠ SINGLE):**
  - The design felt maps uniformly onto `FeltFrame` via `BoardGeometry.fullScale` (crescent 1.22, rect 1.25,
    oval 0.875). Design centre → felt centre (0.5, 0); `TOP` → 14/16 + 1/64.
  - The mapping is held in `BoardState.contentScale/originX/Y/Z` (`footX`/`designX` helpers).
  - Roulette: the design wheel lands on MT's bowl (`wheelCx/Cz`, Ø1.0).
- **Compact table:** the 1 × 1 block-top felt, with content shrunk onto it.
- **`BoardRenderer.feltPose(pose, s)`** applies FACING + the mapping for a game's own extra drawing. Roulette and craps
  call it (one line each; see shared touches).
- **Distances:** the board anchor sits over the dealer row (z −0.5), 0.95 above the felt. LOD and text size measure
  from the felt centre.
- **Plates:** anchored on the padded rail at the seat (`fullPlateAnchor`, y = rail top + 0.03); the leader ends there.
- `SpectatorBlockEntityRenderer.shouldRenderOffScreen` (MT) keeps 3×2 content from being culled.

### Visual QA round 1 (VQA-INWORLD.md §W2)
1. **Felt prints:**
   - New generator `board/art.mjs feltPrint(game, theme)`: one print per game × MT furniture theme (standard / high
     roller / player-banked), 64 px/block over `FeltFrame`.
   - All prints have baize in MT's felt colours, an edge shade (vignette), a 1-px gold `#D8B860` border 3 px inside
     the rail, and a cream rule.
   - Per game:
     - Blackjack: the insurance band, shoe and discard spots.
     - Baccarat: PLAYER / BANKER / TIE / pair boxes in blue, red, green and cream at the renderer's box positions,
       plus both hands' card boxes.
     - UTH: dealer arc, dealer card boxes, 5 community boxes.
     - Hold'em: dashed bet line, 5 community boxes, pot circle, button spot, suit cluster.
     - Craps and roulette: border only (their renderers print the layouts).
   - No baked words, per the project's no-baked-text rule: **localized** felt texts and per-seat spots are drawn at
     runtime by `client/board/FeltPrint`, flat on the felt, in the board font, in the felt's light.
   - Runtime texts:
     - Blackjack: "BLACKJACK PAYS a TO b" (from the `blackjackPayout` config), "INSURANCE PAYS 2 TO 1", and the S17 /
       H17 dealer rule, each curved on its arc.
     - Baccarat: PLAYER / BANKER / TIE n TO 1 / PAIR in the boxes.
     - UTH: "DEALER QUALIFIES WITH A PAIR OR BETTER".
     - Hold'em: POT.
   - Runtime spots:
     - Blackjack: a double ring per seat at `BlackjackWorld.stack(seat)`.
     - UTH: an ante / blind / trips / play group per seat.
   - Strings are in STRINGS `core › In-world board` (`…board.felt.*`, EN+RU), regenerated with `gen_lang`.
2. **Cards:**
   - `CARD_TILT` is now 5° (was 30°) and `CARD_LIFT` 1/64.
   - Full tables draw cards at 0.78 × design size: a seat card is ≈ 0.16 × 0.22 block.
   - Each card has a soft contact shadow that grows and fades with lift.
3. **Plates:** the size cap (above), and the plate and leader are anchored on the rail at the seat.
4. **Ghost plates:** fixed (frame roll); a faded plate reaches alpha 0 and is not drawn below 0.04.
5. **Board from above / near:** it fades out between 55° and 65° camera pitch, and between 1.8 and 1.2 blocks from
   the camera. Its size is capped like the plates'.
6. **Chips:**
   - Piles hold ≤ 5 discs (`CHIP_PILE`); a second pile stands beside the first, up to `CHIP_MAX` = 10 discs.
   - The side band shades darker toward each run's bottom.
   - Each pile has a contact shadow.
7. **Stray yellow "+":** not W2 code. It is most likely the casino attract-mode `chip_glint` particle that
   `worldgen/client/CasinoAttractFx.attract` spawns at main pos y + 1.1 over every table every ~80 ticks (it predates
   W2 and also shows in MT's run). On a 3×2 table at felt height 14/16 it floats above the felt. **Request below.**
8. **Bot tags of neighbouring tables:** hidden at any distance (see M6).
9. **26.3 compile:** fixed (`Blocks.MOSS_CARPET`; dyed carpets are a `ColorCollection` on both versions).
