## R3 (owner playtest 1, 2026-10-20), 26.3 only

### B1: the duel result window re-opened every ~2 min (every PvP mode), fixed server-side
- Cause: an older match leaving the history (`pvp.historyTicks`) or an expiring invite pushed CLOSED, and `PvpUi.clear` wiped
  SENT/OWN of the NEWER match. The 10-tick scan then re-opened its result over any screen.
- `PvpUi.push` of a closed/cancelled match → `closed(p, id)`: it forgets and clears only when the client holds THAT match id.
- A participant is never sent a match that is not their current `matchOf` (e.g. an old duel leaving its rematch window).
- The scan opens each own view (`<id>/<state>`) once (`OPENED`, pruned with the 400-tick cleanup) and never a match in `history`/`closed`.
  The engine's own pushes stay `open=false`; the scan opens what was never opened (a lobby hosted at a machine). OWN was removed.
- Client guard `ClientPvp.CLOSED_RESULTS` (59e613d3) kept.
- Tests: `PvpHistoryGameTests` (own environment `burmaldaholic:pvp_history`; historyTicks 100 / rematch 60 / invite 60, restored):
  two back-to-back scratch duels, both expire, and no `open=true` reaches either player; plus an invite from a third player expiring after the result was closed.

### B4: scratch cards jumped between slots, fixed
- The stack stays where it is. The in-progress card (id, kind, mask, face) is held in `ScratchData` as the player's ACTIVE card
  per kind (`active: <uuid>/<kind> → id`, saved).
- An unfinished card is always resumed, never re-drawn, even when the screen sends an empty id.
- On the ninth cell, one fresh card is shrunk (the held stack first), the card is forgotten, the prize is paid, and a used card goes through `giveOrDrop` (used cards stack).
- If the stack has left the inventory, nothing is revealed (error), and nothing is paid without taking the card.
- Legacy cards (pre-0.2.0, id/mask on a split single) finish the old way.
- Tests (`ExtrasV2GameTests`): `scratchCardsStayInTheirSlot` (stack stays in slot 4; 2 used cards in one stack; each prize paid once) and
  `scratchDisconnectMidCardNoDupeNoLoss` (disconnect mid-card: 3 cards kept, 0 paid, mask saved; rejoin resumes the same id; finish takes 1 card and pays its prize once).
- `JavaReviewGameTests.m1ScratchFaceNeverOnTheItem` was updated: nothing on the item, face server-side. **Shared touch.**
